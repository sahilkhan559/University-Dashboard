##  Project Overview
A comprehensive and interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing **500 University Students**. This dashboard provides a 360-degree view of student enrollment, academic performance, financial status, and demographics to enable data-driven decision making for university management.

**Key Features:**
- Dynamic slicers for Section (A, B, C) and Semester (1st to 8th)
- KPI tracking and trend analysis from 2022 to 2026

## KPIs 
- **Total Students:** 500
- **Total Courses:** 10
- **Average GPA:** 2.76
- **Max GPA:** 4.00

##  Dashboard Breakdown & Insights

### 1. Total Students by City
Mardan leads with **71 students**, followed by Abbottabad (65) & Peshawar (65). Lowest is Swabi (57). Data shows balanced distribution across 8 cities of KP, with Mardan as the major hub.

### 2. Fee Status
- **Paid:** 300 Students (60%) - Healthy cash flow
- **Pending:** 101 Students (20.2%) - Requires follow-up
- **Partial:** 99 Students (19.8%) - On installment plan
> **Insight:** 40% of students have incomplete fee status. An automated fee reminder system can improve collection.

### 3. Yearly Trend (2022-2026)
- 2022: 88, **2023: 116 (Peak Year)**, 2024: 97, 2025: 97, 2026: 102
> **Insight:** Huge growth in 2023 (+31%). After that enrollment is stable. 2023's marketing strategy should be analyzed and replicated.

### 4. Program Wise Distribution
- **BBA:** 103 (Top), BSDS: 95, BSAF: 76, BSIT: 76, BSCS: 75, BSSE: 75
> **Insight:** BBA is the most demanding program (20.6% share). Business and Data Science programs are leading. All programs have strong enrollment (75+).

### 5. Gender Wise
- **Male:** 257 (51.4%), **Female:** 243 (48.6%)
> **Insight:** Excellent gender balance, almost 50-50 ratio shows inclusive admission policy.

### 6. Scholarship
- **No:** 378 (75.6%), **Yes:** 122 (24.4%)
> **Insight:** Only 24.4% students availing scholarship. Opportunity to increase scholarship to attract high GPA (4.00) students.

## Key Recommendations
1. Replicate 2023 admission campaign to cross 116 admissions again
2. Focus on fee recovery - 101 students pending is high
3. Expand faculty for BBA & BSDS due to high demand
4. Start outreach in Swabi & D.I. Khan to balance city-wise distribution
5. Increase scholarship budget from 24% to 35% to improve merit
